About the Studio

I've always been drawn to art, design, and the endless possibilities of creative expression. With a background in art history and a lifelong appreciation for beautiful and unusual things, I've developed a particular fascination with color, pattern, and the way seemingly unrelated elements can come together to create something interesting.

My artwork is largely intuitive. I enjoy experimenting, following an idea wherever it leads, and occasionally surprising myself with the results. I'm not particularly interested in following rules or fitting neatly into one artistic category.

Helmamay Studios is a place for that curiosity to take shape. It's where I explore ideas, play with color and form, and create things simply because I feel compelled to make them.

Above all, I believe art should inspire curiosity, invite conversation, and sometimes make you look twice.

Artwork Tailored to Your Project

Art has the power to transform a space, introduce an unexpected element, and give a room its own personality.

Helmamay Studios works with interior designers to bring distinctive abstract artwork into residential and commercial interiors. Each design is available as a fine-art print, with custom sizing, matting, and framing to suit your vision.

Every fine-art print is personally signed by the artist and accompanied by a Certificate of Authenticity.

Serving interior designers throughout northern New Jersey, the New York metropolitan area, and Connecticut. Local pickup or delivery available by arrangement.
